# Daily Quintet Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

## Introduction

Daily Quintet respects your privacy. We believe your personal goals, tasks, aspirations, and progress should remain yours. Daily Quintet is designed to operate locally on your device without collecting or transmitting your personal information to external servers.

## Information Collection and Use

Daily Quintet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developers or third parties.

Information you enter into the application, including daily tasks, aspirations, milestones, and progress, is stored locally on your device and is used solely to provide the application's functionality.

## Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored locally on your device. Daily Quintet does not operate a user account system or maintain external databases containing your personal information.

The security of locally stored information is also dependent on your device's security settings and protections.

## Third-Party Services

Daily Quintet does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services to collect personal information.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because information is stored locally on your device, it remains there until you delete it or remove the application and its associated data.

Daily Quintet does not retain copies of your personal information on external servers.

Please note that device-level backups may be subject to Apple's own backup and retention settings.

## Children's Privacy

Daily Quintet does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the application evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page, along with an updated effective date.
