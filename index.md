# Daily Quintet

### Make room for what matters.

Daily Quintet is a minimalist productivity app designed to help you be intentional with your time, focus on meaningful priorities, and make consistent progress toward the things that matter to you.

Rather than managing an endless to-do list, Daily Quintet encourages you to identify up to five commitments each day. These are the things you choose to prioritize, give your attention to, and work toward completing.

Not everything needs to happen today. Daily Quintet gives you a place to organize your tasks, ideas, aspirations, and future plans so you can focus on the present without losing sight of the bigger picture.

---

# Getting Started

## How Daily Quintet Works

Daily Quintet is built around a simple idea: you don't need to accomplish everything to make meaningful progress.

### 1. Choose Your Daily Five

At the beginning of your day, identify up to five commitments that deserve your attention.

These can be work responsibilities, personal tasks, habits, or anything else you want to prioritize.

Be intentional about what makes the list. Your five commitments represent what you want to focus on today, not everything you could possibly accomplish.

### 2. Organize Everything Else

Not every task, idea, or aspiration needs to become an immediate priority.

Daily Quintet provides several categories to help you organize the things you want to remember without adding unnecessary pressure to your day.

**Maybe Do**

Things you'd like to accomplish if time permits. These are possibilities, not obligations.

**Could Do**

Optional tasks and ideas that you may want to revisit when the opportunity arises.

**Someday Do**

Longer-term goals, plans, and aspirations that you'd like to work toward in the future.

**Bucket List**

Experiences, adventures, and personal aspirations that you'd love to pursue throughout your life.

### 3. Make Progress

Work through your daily commitments at your own pace. Complete tasks, recognize your progress, and give yourself credit for the things you accomplish.

Progress isn't always about checking everything off a list. It's about consistently directing your attention toward the things that matter.

### 4. Recognize Your Milestones

Meaningful progress deserves recognition.

Use milestones to acknowledge accomplishments and reflect on how far you've come. Small steps can add up to something significant over time.

### 5. Practice Being Your Own Advocate

Daily Quintet also encourages you to approach your goals with a healthier perspective.

Use personal affirmations as reminders to support yourself, recognize your efforts, and be intentional about how you approach each day.

You don't have to do everything today. You just have to decide what matters today.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to complete all five daily commitments?

No. The five commitments represent your priorities, not a requirement to accomplish everything. They are intended to help you direct your attention toward what matters most.

### What happens to tasks I don't complete?

You can reconsider your priorities and decide what deserves your attention on another day. Not completing something doesn't mean you've failed.

### What's the difference between Maybe Do, Could Do, and Someday Do?

These categories help distinguish between things you might do when time permits, optional ideas you want to revisit, and longer-term goals you're interested in pursuing.

### Does Daily Quintet require an account?

No. Daily Quintet is designed to be used without creating an account.

### Is my information stored online?

No. Your information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers by Daily Quintet.

Daily Quintet respects your privacy. We believe your personal goals, tasks, aspirations, and progress should remain yours.

Daily Quintet does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developers or third parties.
